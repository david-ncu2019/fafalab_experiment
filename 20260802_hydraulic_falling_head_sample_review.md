# Hydraulic Falling-Head Sample Review

The present sieve results should be treated as screening data, not final soil texture. The samples are Yunlin paddy soils, so high apparent sand content may come from dried silt-clay aggregates that were not fully dispersed before sieving. The tables below focus on which samples to try for falling-head testing and which samples should be repeated first.

## Repeat Priority

Highest priority for repeat preparation:

1. `Sample_1-9`: most suspicious because it appears too clean and sandy.
2. `Sample_8-7-8`: highest fines in Site 8.
3. `Sample_8-5-6`: high fines, likely poor falling-head behavior.
4. `Sample_4_5-6`: high fines, not a good first falling-head target.

For repeated sieve preparation, gently break clods without crushing true mineral grains, soak with sodium hexametaphosphate, wet-wash through #200 until the wash water is nearly clear, then dry and sieve the retained fraction. If silt-clay separation matters, run hydrometer or another fine-particle method on the material passing #200.
